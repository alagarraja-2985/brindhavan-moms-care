Our story

Care begins with people who care.

Brindhavan Elder Moms' Care grew from a heartfelt belief: every elderly mother deserves dignity, companionship and a place where she feels at home.

“No mother should have to face later life feeling alone.”

Inception

An idea born of love

Brindhavan Elder Moms' Care is the brainchild of Ms. Parimala Rajasekar, a retired Technical Officer (Gazetted Rank) from CVRDE, DRDO of India.

After a career dedicated to national service, she turned her energy toward a deeply personal cause: the dignity and happiness of elderly mothers. The initiative is a joint venture made possible with the support of her family.

EMC was created so elderly single mothers can live among peers and caring staff in an atmosphere of warmth, respect and belonging.

Our vision

A trusted home for every mother.

To be a trusted home where every elderly single mother lives with dignity, security and joy—never alone and never without care.

Our mission

To provide a safe, comfortable and loving place to live, with healthy meals, 24/7 caretaker support, regular mental and health care, and swift emergency medical help through a hospital tie-up. We aim to keep mothers active and engaged through yoga, motivational talks and music.

“Every mother is more than a resident. She is part of our extended family.”
