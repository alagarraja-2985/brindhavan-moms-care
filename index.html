Our purpose

More than a residence. A place to belong.

Founded from a deeply personal commitment to the dignity and happiness of elderly mothers, Brindhavan EMC aims to make sure no mother has to face later life in isolation.

  • A safe, comfortable and welcoming living environment
  • Regular health monitoring and emotional support
  • Companionship, activities and a caring community
  • Emergency medical support through a hospital tie-up
